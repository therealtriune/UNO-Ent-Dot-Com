North West & Molly Santana’s Tour Gets Shelved Without Explanation

North West just pulled the plug on her first headlining tour, but she’s already got something brewing behind the scenes that’s got fans on edge. The 13-year-old daughter of Kim Kardashian and Kanye West was supposed to hit the road with rapper Molly Santana for the Kimokawaii Tour starting August 5 in Dallas, but the entire 14-date North American run has been shelved without explanation. The cancellation came quietly at first, with tour dates vanishing from ticketing platforms one by one.
