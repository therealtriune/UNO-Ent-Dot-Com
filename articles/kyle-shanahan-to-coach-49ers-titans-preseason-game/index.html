Kyle Shanahan to coach 49ers-Titans preseason game

SANTA CLARA, CA - MAY 8: Head Coach Kyle Shanahan of the San Francisco 49ers on the field during Rookie Minicamp at SAP Performance Facility on May 8, 2026 in Santa Clara, California. (Photo by Michael Zagaris/San Francisco 49ers/Getty Images) | Getty Images San Francisco 49ers head coach Kyle Shanahan took to the podium for the first time in training camp to answer questions, and fans may get to see him a bit more frequently. In addition to answering questions regarding the car accident that led to his absence to start camp, Shanahan said he will be coaching the 49ers’ preseason game against the Tennessee Titans on Thursday, August 13.
