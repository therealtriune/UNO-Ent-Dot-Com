Jacquees Says It Would Have Been ‘Dope’ To Be A Part Of Usher & Chris Brown’s Tour, But Refused To Sing ‘Background’ Like ‘Mario And Tank’

Welp, it looks like Jacquees is making crystal clear to the culture that he’s not trading his spot in the spotlight to play second fiddle to anyone… even R&B royalty. Sitting down on Streetz Morning Takeover , the self-proclaimed “King of R&B” pulled back the curtain on why fans won’t be seeing him on stage for the highly anticipated lineup alongside heavyweights like Chris Brown and Usher. While the internet had been speculating his absence, Jacquees admitted he’d rather stay in his own lane than step down into a supporting role.
