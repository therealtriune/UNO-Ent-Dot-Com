Jan Blachowicz reacts to UFC Belgrade loss: 'It is what it is'

UFC Fight Night 283 did not mark the resurgence of former light heavyweight champion Jan Blachowicz. Instead, it marked the continuance of his late-career slide. Blachowicz (29-12-2 MMA, 12-9-2 UFC) saw his winless skid hit five fightsSaturday when he suffered a first-round TKO loss to Navajo Stirling (11-0 MMA, 6-0 UFC) in their co-main event showdown at Belgrade Arena in Serbia.
