UFC Belgrade video: Michael Oliveira touted as potential star after debut KO

Michael Oliveira had perhaps the most anticipated of the nine debuts at UFC Fight Night 283 , and he delivered against Oban Elliott. Oliveira (10-0 MMA, 1-0 UFC) found his range early in the welterweight bout with Elliott (12-5 MMA, 3-3 UFC) on Saturday at Belgrade Arena in Serbia. He scored a quick knockdown courtesy of the jab, then shortly after got another knockdown with an overhand left, leading to swarm of ground strikes for the knockout at the 1:49 mark of Round 1.
