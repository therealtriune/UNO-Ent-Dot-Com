UFC Belgrade video: Robert Valentin chokes Dusko Todorovic, makes him translate speech

Robert Valentin continued to crawl out of his bad octagon start on Saturday with another win, this time against Dusko Todorovic at UFC Fight Night 283 . After starting his promotional tenure with three consecutive defeats, Valentin (12-6 MMA, 2-3 UFC) now has consecutive wins on his record after he went into Belgrade Arena and upset Serbia's own Todorovic (13-7 MMA, 4-7 UFC) with a guillotine choke submission at the 4:14 mark of Round 1. The crowd was "muted," in the words of play-by-play man Jon Anik after the local favorite lost in the middleweight bout, but Valentin achieved a successful night.
