Roster news: NY Giants place DT Roy Robertson-Harris on injured reserve

﻿New York Giants defensive tackle Roy Robertson-Harris celebrates with pass rusher Brian Burns after sacking Las Vegas Raiders quarterback Geno Smith | Getty Images The New York Giants made four roster moves prior to practice on Wednesday, 8/5. The Giants announced that they’ve placed defensive tackle Roy Robertson-Harris on the injured reserve, as well as waiving wide receiver Beaux Collins with an injury designation. Those two moves create a pair of needed roster openings after the team signed cornerbacks David Long and DJ James .
