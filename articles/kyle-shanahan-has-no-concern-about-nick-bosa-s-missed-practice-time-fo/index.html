Kyle Shanahan has no concern about Nick Bosa's missed practice time for soreness

49ers defensive end Nick Bosa, who missed most of last season with a torn ACL, has been absent from practice recently due to soreness. In coach Kyle Shanahan's first training-camp press conference, he was asked whether the situation causes any concern. “No," Shanahan said.
