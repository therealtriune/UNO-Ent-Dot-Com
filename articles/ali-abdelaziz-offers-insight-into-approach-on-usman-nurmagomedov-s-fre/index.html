Ali Abdelaziz offers insight into approach on Usman Nurmagomedov's free agency

PFL lightweight champion Usman Nurmagomedov is the hottest free agent in MMA. Nurmagomedov (22-0) completed the final fight of his contract when he knocked out Archie Colgan in the first-round of this past Friday's PFL New York main event at UBS Arena in Elmont. Ali Abdelaziz of Dominance MMA, who manages Nurmagomedov, said PFL opted not to make an offer before Nurmagomedov fought out his contract.
