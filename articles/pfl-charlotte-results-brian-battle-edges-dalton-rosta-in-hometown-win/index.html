PFL Charlotte results: Brian Battle edges Dalton Rosta in hometown win

Bryan Battle's hometown showdown was a success. Battle, winner of season 29 of The Ultimate Fighter, picked up his first win under the PFL banner by beating Dalton Rosta in the main event of PFL Charlotte, which took place Friday at Bojangles Coliseum in North Carolina. Battle (13-3) defeated Rosta (11-4) in a split decision that read 29-28 on all three judges' scorecards – two in favor of Battle and one for Rosta.
