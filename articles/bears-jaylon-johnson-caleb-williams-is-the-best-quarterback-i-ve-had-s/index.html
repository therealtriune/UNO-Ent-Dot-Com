Bears' Jaylon Johnson: Caleb Williams is the best quarterback I've had since Nick Foles

Chicago cornerback Jaylon Johnson has been with the Bears since they drafted him in 2020, and he's faced a lot of quarterbacks on the practice field during that time. Perhaps surprisingly, he doesn't necessarily view Caleb Williams as the best of those quarterbacks. In what might be considered damning with faint praise, Johnson talked up the Bears' offense when asked about training camp practices, but he suggested that Williams isn't the best Bears quarterback he's practiced against, just the best since Nick Foles was in Chicago during Johnson's first two seasons.
