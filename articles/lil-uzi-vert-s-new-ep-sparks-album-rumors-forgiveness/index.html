Lil Uzi Vert’s New EP Sparks Album Rumors & Forgiveness

Lil Uzi Vert found a way get his fans back after disappointment was seemingly taking over. Fans gathered at Lollapalooza only to learn the star would not be performing, Uzi came back with something great: brand new music. The surprise EP “Maverick Almost Forever” was birthed Thursday/Friday (basically July 31) and also celebrated his 31st birthday with the eight-song project.
