Ditcheva outclasses Kielholtz on return from injury

Dakota Ditcheva outclassed Denise Kielholtz as the Briton marked her return from injury with a comfortable win by unanimous decision at PFL New York. Ditcheva, who had not fought since breaking her left hand just over a year ago, extended the unbeaten start to her career to 16 fights. The win for Ditcheva, who triumphed in the PFL flyweight tournament in 2024 , was a timely reminder to fans of why she is one of Britain's most talented and exciting fighters.
