UFC Belgrade: Dennis Buzukja protests tap to Bogdan Grad's choke

Bogdan Grad had too much pace, control and output for Dennis Buzukja at UFC Fight Night 283 , and it led to a stoppage win much to the delight of an electric crowd in Serbia. Grad (16-4 MMA, 2-2 UFC) largely had his way with Buzukja (12-7 MMA, 1-5 UFC) due to a stronger grappling game in the featherweight bout at Belgrade Arena. He got into top position in the second round and secured an arm-triangle choke that forced referee Jason Herzog to jump in and halt the action.
