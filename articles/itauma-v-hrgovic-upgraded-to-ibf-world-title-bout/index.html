Itauma v Hrgovic upgraded to IBF world-title bout

Moses Itauma (left) has described Filip Hrgovic as his "toughest fight" to date [Getty Images] British heavyweight Moses Itauma's fight against Filip Hrgovic on 29 August will be for the vacant IBF title. Itauma, 21, has been widely tipped as the next star of heavyweight boxing, with 12 knockouts in 14 professional victories. Promoter Frank Warren told BBC Sport the IBF belt, vacated by Oleksandr Usyk in June, will be on the line at London's O2 Arena.
