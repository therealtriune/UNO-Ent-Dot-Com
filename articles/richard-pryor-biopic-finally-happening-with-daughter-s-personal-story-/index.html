Richard Pryor Biopic Finally Happening With Daughter’s Personal Story As Source

Richard Pryor is finally getting his moment on the big screen, and it’s coming straight from his daughter’s deeply personal story. Latigo Films and Inaugural Entertainment just locked down the rights to “Something We Said: Richard Pryor, a Notorious Word and Me,” Elizabeth Stordeur Pryor’s memoir that digs into the complicated relationship between one of comedy’s greatest minds and the man who struggled to be her father. This isn’t just another Hollywood biopic trying to cash in on a legend’s name.
