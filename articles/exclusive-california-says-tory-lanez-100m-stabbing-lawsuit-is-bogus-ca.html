EXCLUSIVE: California Says Tory Lanez $100M Stabbing Lawsuit Is Bogus & Can’t Be Filed Under Federal Law

Tory Lanez better be ready, because The California prison system is fighting back against his $100 million stabbing lawsuit. California’s Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation filed a motion to dismiss his federal complaint on July 29 arguing federal courts have no power over a state agency. That lawsuit grew out of a violent attack on Tory Lanez that took place on May 12, 2025 inside a Tehachapi prison.
