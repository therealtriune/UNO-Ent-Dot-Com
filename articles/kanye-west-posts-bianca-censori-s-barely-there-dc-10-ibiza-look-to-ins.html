Kanye West Posts Bianca Censori’s Barely-There DC-10 Ibiza Look to Instagram

Bianca Censori turned the DC-10 nightclub in Ibiza into her personal runway, wearing what appeared at first to be nothing but thigh-high boots. Kanye West posted the images on Instagram, showing Censori in a barely-there sheer bodysuit that created the illusion of nudity from a distance. Upon closer inspection, the 31-year-old Australian designer was wearing a sheer, skin-toned bodysuit that covered her just enough to keep the posts live.
