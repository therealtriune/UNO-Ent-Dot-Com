Stefon Diggs Wins Major Victory As Judge Demands Accuser’s Evidence

Stefon Diggs is facing mounting legal pressure as his battle with accuser Christopher Griffith intensifies through court-ordered discovery demands that are forcing both sides to reveal their evidence. The NFL star’s legal team just won a major procedural victory when a judge ordered Griffith to hand over every direct message he’s exchanged with Diggs on Instagram within the next two weeks, along with comprehensive documentation of his travel to Maryland during the alleged incident. This escalation marks a critical turning point in a case that’s been building since late 2025.
