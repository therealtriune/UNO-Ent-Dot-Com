UFC Fight Night 284 weigh-in results, live video stream

LAS VEGAS – MMA Junkie is on scene and reporting live from Friday's UFC Fight Night 284 (Paramount+) fighter weigh-ins at the Meta APEX in Las Vegas, which kick off at noon ET (9 a.m. PT). Among those weighing in are lightweight contenders Mateusz Gamrot (26-4 MMA, 9-4 UFC) and Quillan Salkilld (12-1 MMA, 5-0 UFC), who meet in the main event, and Diego Ferreira (20-7 MMA, 11-7 UFC) and Billy Quarantillo (20-8 MMA, 6-5 UFC), who fight in the lightweight co-feature.
