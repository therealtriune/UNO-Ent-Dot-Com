Big U Charged With Trying To Murder Witnesses From Jail

Big U is facing seven fresh federal charges that paint a picture of a man willing to orchestrate violence from behind bars to silence witnesses against him. The Rollin’ 60s Neighborhood Crips leader and music executive Eugene Henley Jr., 59, was hit with the new indictment on Wednesday, and prosecutors say he’s been soliciting fellow inmates to murder or seriously injure a federal witness who’s set to testify at his trial. This isn’t just another charge in an already massive case.
