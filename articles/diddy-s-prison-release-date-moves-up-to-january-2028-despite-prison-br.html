Diddy’s Prison Release Date Moves Up To January 2028 Despite Prison Brawl

Diddy’s release date from federal prison just moved up again despite him sitting in solitary confinement after a fight at Fort Dix FCI. The BOP website now lists January 24, 2028 as his projected exit date, a month ahead of where the number sat before the fight. He was tossed in the hole last week after a fellow inmate dissed him and things turned physical.
