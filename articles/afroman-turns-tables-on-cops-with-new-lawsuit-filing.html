Afroman Turns Tables On Cops With New Lawsuit Filing

Afroman is turning the tables on law enforcement with a lawsuit of his own, taking direct aim at the officer he says stole from him during a controversial 2022 raid. The rapper announced the legal action on his birthday via Instagram, making it clear he’s not backing down from his battle with the Adams County Sheriff’s Office. This move marks a dramatic shift in the narrative, transforming him from defendant to plaintiff in a saga that’s captivated the internet for years.
