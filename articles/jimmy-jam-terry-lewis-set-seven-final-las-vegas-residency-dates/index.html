Jimmy Jam & Terry Lewis Set Seven Final Las Vegas Residency Dates

Jimmy Jam & Terry Lewis are bringing their catalog back to Las Vegas for seven final shows, closing their first residency with serious R&B firepower. According to The Venetian’s listing , “Nothing But Hits” returns to Voltaire on September 23, 25, 26 and 30, followed by October 1, 2 and 3. “Bringing ‘Nothing But Hits’ back to the stage to finish our first Vegas residency is such an exciting opportunity and we look forward to creating even more memories from songs that shaped generations of music fans,” Jimmy Jam said.
