MGK Day 2026 Is Back With Wiz Khalifa And A Mission To Give Back To Cleveland

MGK is bringing the biggest MGK Day yet to Cleveland this summer and Wiz Khalifa is rolling with him for the ride. The two artists will co-headline the MGK Day Art + Community Festival on August 8 at North Coast Yard in Cleveland, according to Billboard , with gates opening at 3 P.M. and the main stage firing up at 6 P.M.
