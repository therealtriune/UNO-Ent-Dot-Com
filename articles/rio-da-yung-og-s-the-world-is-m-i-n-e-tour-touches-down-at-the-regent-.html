Rio Da Yung OG’s “The World is M.I.N.E.” Tour Touches Down at the Regent Theater

The Regent Theater in Los Angeles is set to become the epicenter of raw, unfiltered hip-hop on August 4th as Flint, Michigan’s grittiest export, Rio Da Yung OG , rolls through as part of his highly anticipated “The World is M.I.N.E.” Tour. For those who’ve been locked into his wave, Rio—born Da’mario Donshay Horne-McCullough—needs no introduction. He’s the rapper who turned the mundane miseries of street life into hypnotic, off-kilter poetry, delivering bars with a deadpan stare and a darkly comedic twist that catches you off guard every single time.
