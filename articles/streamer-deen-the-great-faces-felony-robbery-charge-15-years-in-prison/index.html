Streamer Deen The Great Faces Felony Robbery Charge & 15 Years In Prison

Deen The Great is facing legal trouble that could carry significant consequences after Florida prosecutors charged the popular streamer with felony robbery in connection with an alleged confrontation aboard a yacht in Miami. The former boxer faces up to 15 years in prison if convicted on felony robbery charges. Deen, whose legal name is Nurideen Shabazz, was also charged with misdemeanor battery stemming from an alleged incident on May 27 involving a woman, according to court documents.
