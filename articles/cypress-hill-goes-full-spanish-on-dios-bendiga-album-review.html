Cypress Hill Goes Full Spanish on “Dios Bendiga” (Album Review)

South Gate, California veterans Cypress Hill going full Spanish for their 11th studio LP. Consisting of B-Real & Sen Dog on the mic as well as DJ Muggs on the boards & Eric Bobo on percussion, the quartet would take the west coast by storm a little over 3 decades back off their eponymous debut under Ruffhouse Records in tandem with Columbia Records because of Muggs’ unique production style & their pro-marijuana legalization themes. Other standouts in the group’s discography include Black Sunday , my personal favorite Cypress Hill III: Temples of Boom , the criminally underappreciated Cypress Hill IV , Elephants on Acid & the Black Milk -produced Back in Black through MNRK Music Group.
