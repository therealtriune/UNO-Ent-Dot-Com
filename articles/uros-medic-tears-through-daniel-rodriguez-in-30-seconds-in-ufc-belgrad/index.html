Uros Medic tears through Daniel Rodriguez in 30 seconds in UFC Belgrade main event

Daniel Rodriguez landed a hard low kick that took Uros Medic off his feet, and about 10 seconds later it was all over. Problem was, for Rodriguez, he was seeing stars from the canvas hearing a sold-out crowd blow the proverbial roof off the place for Medic. The home-country hero headliner recovered from the kick, drilled Rodriguez (20-6 MMA, 10-5 UFC) with a left to the chin, followed by a couple punches and a knee, and a few seconds later Medic (14-3 MMA, 8-3 UFC) had a 30-second knockout.
