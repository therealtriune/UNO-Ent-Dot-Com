Lions agree to terms with LB Devin White

The Lions have agreed to terms with free agent linebacker Devin White on a one-year deal, according to Jordan Schultz of The Schultz Report. White, 28, is entering his eighth season. He spent last season with the Raiders and started all 17 games, totaling 174 tackles, 2.5 sacks, seven quarterback hits, one interception, three forced fumbles and one pass breakup.
