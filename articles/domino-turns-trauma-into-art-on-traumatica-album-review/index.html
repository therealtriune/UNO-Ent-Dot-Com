Domino Turns Trauma into Art on 'Traumatica' (Album Review)

Independent hip-hop artist Domino has transformed one of life's most devastating moments into a powerful 10-track concept album with Traumatica , a deeply personal project documenting his recovery from a traumatic brain injury sustained on October 17, 2025. The album, released July 28, 2026 via Flourish Gazette, chronicles a journey that left the multidisciplinary artist unable to look at screens, make music, or even recognize his own face for months — all while $15K in debt stacked up from surviving it. But somewhere in that silence, between Beethoven on low and vestibular exercises, something shifted.
