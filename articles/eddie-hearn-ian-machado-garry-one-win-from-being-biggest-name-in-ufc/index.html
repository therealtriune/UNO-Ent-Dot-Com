Eddie Hearn: Ian Machado Garry one win from being ‘biggest name in UFC’

Eddie Hearn believes that Ian Machado Garry is one win from being “arguably the biggest name in the UFC ”. On 15 August, Machado Garry will challenge Islam Makhachev for the welterweight title, as the pair headline UFC 330 in Philadelphia . The bout represents a stern test for the Irish challenger, 28, as he takes on the UFC’s pound-for-pound No 1 fighter .
