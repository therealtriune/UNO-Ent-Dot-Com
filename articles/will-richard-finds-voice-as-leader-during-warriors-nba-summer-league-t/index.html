Will Richard finds voice as leader during Warriors' NBA Summer League title run

Will Richard finds voice as leader during Warriors' NBA Summer League title run originally appeared on NBC Sports Bay Area LAS VEGAS – Will Richard’s goal going into his second NBA Summer League was to build off what he was best at as a rookie for the Warriors: Cutting, hitting open shots and guarding at a high level. Sitting at the Chase Center podium for Warriors Summer League Media Day at the end of June, Richard then listed another area of his game he was focused on. “I want to improve my communication,” Richard said.
