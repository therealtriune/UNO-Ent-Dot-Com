Irish boxer Reeves 'making progress' in hospital

Lee Reeves was hospitalised after his knockout defeat by Gary Cully on Saturday night [Getty Images] Queensberry promoter Frank Warren has said that Irish boxer Lee Reeves is "making progress" after being hospitalised following his fight in Dublin on Saturday night. The 31-year-old's condition was described as "critical but stable" after he was admitted for treatment following his defeat to Gary Cully. Reeves was fighting for the vacant BUI Celtic super-lightweight title but was knocked out in the 10th round at the 3Arena.
