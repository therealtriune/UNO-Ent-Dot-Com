Jay-Z Says Tony Buzbee Misled Client & Court In New Legal Filing

A little-known New York statute that allows attorneys to be sued for intentionally deceiving a court has become the latest battleground in Jay-Z ’s legal fight against Texas lawyer Tony Buzbee. AllHipHop reports that the rapper and business mogul has asked a federal judge in the Southern District of New York for permission to file a third amended complaint, adding a claim under New York Judiciary Law Section 487. The law, which is infrequently invoked, permits civil claims against attorneys accused of deliberately misleading either the court or parties involved in litigation.
