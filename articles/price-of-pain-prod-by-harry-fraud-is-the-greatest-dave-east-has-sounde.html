“Price of Pain” Prod. by Harry Fraud is the Greatest Dave East Has Sounded Since Leaving Def Jam (Album Review)

Harlem, New York emcee Dave East reuniting with Harry Fraud for his 6th solo LP. Breaking out in 2014 off his 8th mixtape Black Rose , this resulted in the man signing a joint deal with Def Jam Recordings & even Nas ’ independently owned Mass Appeal Records as well as a spot in the iconic 2016 XXL Freshman Class. However, his full-length debut Survival wouldn’t come out until 3 years later & was very disappointing in the sense that he tried appealing to a more mainstream audience that just didn’t exist.
