UFC Belgrade results: Uros Medic sets off a party in Serbia, smashes Daniel Rodriguez in 30 seconds

Uros Medic ensured Saturday will be a night Serbian MMA won't soon forget. On an evening where the UFC established new records for the most total finishes (12) and first-round finishes (10) on a single event, Serbia's Medic ended the festivities with the finest performance of his UFC career, knocking out Daniel Rodriguez in just 30 seconds to set off a raucous party inside the Belgrade Arena. Medic (14-3) caught Rodriguez clean with a wicked left hand in the opening seconds, then downed the welterweight veteran with a follow-up combination and sealed the deal with a vicious salvo of strikes.
