iShowSpeed’s New Content Studio Is a 26,600 Sq Ft All-Black Warehouse Near Miami

iShowSpeed just dropped nearly $11 million on a Miami-Dade warehouse he’s converting into his full-scale content creation studio. The 26,600-square-foot industrial building sits at 8700 Northwest Seventh Avenue on 1.8 acres in unincorporated Miami-Dade County, according to The Real Deal . The property sits near West Little River and has already been painted completely black, windows and all.
