UFC Vegas 120 Fighter Explains Late Withdrawal

UFC Vegas 120 Getty Jessie Rosas Instagram Stories 1/2 Heavy Jessie Rosas Instagram Stories 2/2 Heavy UFC Vegas 120 fighter Jessie Rosas, who was set to make his UFC debut, explained why he was forced to withdraw from the card at late notice. As first reported by Heavy.com’s Marcel Dorff, Rosas was forced to pull out of his featherweight fight for Saturday’s UFC Vegas 120 card against Miles Johns. In his place steps in Gianni Vazquez, who returns to the UFC on extremely short notice after losing to Javid Basharat in his promotional debut earlier this year and immediately being released due to missing weight.
