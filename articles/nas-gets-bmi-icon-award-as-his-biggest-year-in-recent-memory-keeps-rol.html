Nas Gets BMI Icon Award As His Biggest Year In Recent Memory Keeps Rolling

Nas is set to receive the BMI Icon Award at the 2026 BMI R&B/Hip-Hop Awards on August 26 in Los Angeles. BMI announced the honor today, recognizing Nas as one of rap’s greatest storytellers and a cultural force impossible to measure. The private ceremony in LA will also honor the songwriters, producers, and publishers behind the year’s 35 most-performed R&B/Hip-Hop songs from BMI’s catalog.
