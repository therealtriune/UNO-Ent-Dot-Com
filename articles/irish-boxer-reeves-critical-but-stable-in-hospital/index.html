Irish boxer Reeves 'critical but stable' in hospital

Lee Reeves was hospitalised after his knockout defeat by Gary Cully in Dublin on Saturday [Getty Images] Irish boxer Lee Reeves is in a "critical but stable" condition in hospital after he was stretchered out of the ring in Dublin on Saturday. Reeves was facing Gary Cully for the vacant BUI Celtic super-lightweight title but was knocked out in the 10th round at the 3Arena. The 31-year-old Limerick fighter was stretchered out of the ring after the fight and transferred to hospital.
