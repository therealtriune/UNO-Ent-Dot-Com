Wakko the Kidd & the Insane Clown Posse Tell the Legend of “Bizerker 187”

Psychopathic Records & Juggalo Championship Wrestling (JCW) founders the Insane Clown Posse have joined the label that runs beneath the streets’ most recent signing Wakko the Kidd for his brand new single “Bizerker 187” produced by Devereaux . The west coast artist & entrepreneur is fresh off his brand new EP The Kidd That Did from earlier this month, handling all the performances by himself as opposed to the feature-heavy In Airheadz We Trust back in January.
