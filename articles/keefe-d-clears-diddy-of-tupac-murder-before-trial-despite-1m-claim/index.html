Keefe D Clears Diddy Of Tupac Murder Before Trial Despite $1M Claim

Keefe D now says Diddy had no role in Tupac Shakur’s murder, reversing a claim prosecutors may use during his trial. The denial came during an exclusive jailhouse interview provided to 8 News Now , days before jury selection begins in Las Vegas. Reporter Vanessa Murphy asked whether Sean “Diddy” Combs had anything to do with Tupac’s September 1996 killing.
