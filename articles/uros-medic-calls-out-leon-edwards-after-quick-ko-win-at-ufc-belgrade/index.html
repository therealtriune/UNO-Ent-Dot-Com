Uros Medic calls out Leon Edwards after quick KO win at UFC Belgrade

Uros Medic has his eyes set on a former UFC champion. Medic (14-3 MMA, 8-3 UFC) called out Leon Edwards after his quick KO win this Saturday in the UFC's debut event in Serbia. Medic got a 30-second stoppage over veteran Daniel Rodriguez (20-6 MMA, 10-5 UFC) in the main event of UFC Fight Night 282 in Belgrade to extend his win streak to 4-0.
