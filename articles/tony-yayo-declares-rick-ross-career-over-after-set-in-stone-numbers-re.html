Tony Yayo Declares Rick Ross Career Over After “Set In Stone” Numbers Released

Tony Yayo just delivered a brutal assessment of one of Hip-Hop’s most decorated careers, and the numbers backing his critique are impossible to ignore. During a recent podcast appearance, the G-Unit veteran didn’t hold back when discussing Rick Ross’s latest album performance, pointing to first-week sales that fell short of expectations and even underperformed compared to artists currently facing legal troubles. The conversation quickly escalated beyond just album numbers into a broader commentary about legacy, relevance, and what it takes to maintain status in an industry that moves faster than most can keep up with.
