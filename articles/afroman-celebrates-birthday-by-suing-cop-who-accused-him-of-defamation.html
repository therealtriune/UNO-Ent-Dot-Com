Afroman Celebrates Birthday By Suing Cop Who Accused Him Of Defamation

Afroman earned a huge victory for free speech a few months ago when he won his viral defamation case. Ohio deputies were suing him for defamation after making numerous humorous songs following what appeared to be an unethical raid on his home. One of the men suing him was former Adams County Sheriff’s Office sergeant Brian Newland.
