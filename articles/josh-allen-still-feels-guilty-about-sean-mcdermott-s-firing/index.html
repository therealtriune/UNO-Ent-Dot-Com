Josh Allen still feels guilty about Sean McDermott's firing

The last of 10 head-coaching vacancies in the 2026 cycle arose when the Bills surprisingly fired Sean McDermott after a divisional-round overtime loss to the Broncos. Quarterback Josh Allen, who was heavily involved in the search for McDermott's successor, still feels guilty about McDermott's demise, seven months later. “ I think about that all the time ," Allen told Mike Silver of The Athletic .
