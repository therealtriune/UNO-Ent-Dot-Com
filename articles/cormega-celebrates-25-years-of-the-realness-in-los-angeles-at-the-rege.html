Cormega Celebrates 25 Years of The Realness in Los Angeles at the Regent Theater

Cormega On Friday, July 24, legendary Queensbridge rapper Cormega brought the 25th anniversary celebration of his landmark debut album, The Realness , to Los Angeles. Originally released in 2001, the album remains one of the most revered independent hip-hop releases of its era, and fans packed the venue to witness a special performance honoring its legacy. Cormega performed The Realness from front to back, taking the audience on a journey through one of Queensbridge’s defining classics.
