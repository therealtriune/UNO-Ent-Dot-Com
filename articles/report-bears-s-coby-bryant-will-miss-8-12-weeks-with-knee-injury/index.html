Report: Bears S Coby Bryant will miss 8-12 weeks with knee injury

Bears safety Coby Bryant, who was carted off from practice Monday, was diagnosed with a hyperextended left knee, bone bruise and small fracture, Adam Schefter of ESPN reports. Bryant is expected to miss 8-10 weeks. He was one of the team's key offseason signings, joining the Bears on a three-year, $40 million contract.
