Barney-Smith wants to 'mix it with the best'

Royston Barney-Smith beat Reece Bellotti in Dublin on Saturday [Leigh Dawney / Queensberry] British and Commonwealth super-featherweight champion Royston Barney-Smith has ambitions to "mix it with the best" at 130lbs after an impressive win in Dublin. The 22-year-old from Southampton put on a classy performance on Saturday as he beat Reece Bellotti at the 3Arena on the undercard of Mark Chamberlain's win against Pierce O'Leary. "Sugar Boy Roy" dominated "The Bomber" over 10 rounds, boxing well off the back foot as Bellotti came forward and picking his opponent apart at times with ease.
