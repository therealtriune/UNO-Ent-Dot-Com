Navajo Stirling takes out Jan Blachowicz in UFC Belgrade co-main event

A light heavyweight clash between a former champion and an undefeated rising star served as Saturday's UFC Belgrade co-main event. Former titleholder Jan Blachowicz faced the unbeaten Navajo Stirling at Belgrade Arena. Stirling picked up the biggest win of his career and kept his undefeated record intact.
